Four ways to get coffee from us

Everything is small batch by design, we would rather do a few things properly than run a long menu.

The Counter

Espresso & filter bar

Espresso drinks, one rotating single origin filter, and cold brew once the mornings warm up. Cups from R28.

Pastry counter

A short, changing selection baked locally each morning, usually gone by mid morning on weekends.

Beyond the Counter

Bean subscription

A standing order of house or guest roast beans, ground or whole, dropped off or held for collection.

Small batch catering

Filter urns and a pastry box for meetings or early gatherings nearby, up to about 30 people.

Private early opening

Book the counter before normal hours for a shoot, a small event, or a group that needs the space to itself.
